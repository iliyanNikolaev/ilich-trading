PRICE ACTION · LESSON 09CONTINUATION

Continuation Patterns

These patterns describe a pause or brief pullback within an existing move. They may suggest trend continuation, but direction should be confirmed by a breakout and candle close.

FlagPennantThree MethodsInside Bar
01

What should come before the pattern?

Without a preceding impulse, there is nothing to continue.

1 · IMPULSE

Clear move

Price moves clearly in one direction.

2 · PAUSE

Pause or pullback

The move briefly weakens or contracts.

3 · BREAKOUT

Breakout

Price breaks out of the pattern and attempts to continue the impulse.

02

Rising Three Methods

A bullish pattern that may signal continuation to the upside.

  • First candle: a strong green (bullish) candle.
  • It is followed by several smaller red or mixed candles that mostly remain within the first candle’s range.
  • The final candle is strong and green, closing above the consolidation.
What are you waiting for?

The setup requires an uptrend → a brief pause → a close above the range. Do not buy simply because you see a few small red candles.

03

Falling Three Methods

The bearish version – possible continuation to the downside.

  • First candle: a strong red (bearish) candle.
  • It is followed by several smaller green or mixed candles within the first candle’s range.
  • The final candle is strong and red, closing below the consolidation.
What are you waiting for?

Downtrend → a brief rise or pause → a close below the range. Do not sell in the middle of the consolidation.

04

Flag

A strong impulse followed by a channel sloping against the trend.

Bullish flag

A strong upward move followed by a pullback or sideways movement within a slightly descending channel. An upside breakout may confirm continuation.

Bearish flag

A strong downward move followed by a recovery within a slightly rising channel. A downside breakout may confirm continuation.

05

Pennant

A brief consolidation in which the price range gradually narrows.

After the impulse, highs become lower and lows become higher, causing the range to contract. The breakout may follow the previous impulse, but false breakouts also occur.

06

Inside Bar as a pause

The second candle is completely within the first candle’s range.

An Inside Bar shows a temporary contraction in range. Traders often watch for a break above the mother candle’s High or below its Low. The pattern itself does not predict which side will break.

07

Practical rules

  1. Identify the impulse.Its direction is your starting point.
  2. Mark the pause range.Mark the upper and lower boundaries.
  3. Wait for a candle to close outside the range.A wick outside the pattern followed by a close back inside may be a false breakout.
  4. Check the available room for the stop and target.Do not enter if there is not enough room between the breakout and the next important zone.
Do not chase a move that has already run

If the breakout is strong and price does not return to a reasonable entry area, do not chase it. Wait for a new setup.
