SMT Divergence

SMT compares the movement of two related markets. If one makes a new high or low while the other fails to confirm the move, the divergence may suggest weakening momentum.

CorrelationDivergenceConfluence
01

What do we compare?

Use markets with a logical relationship, not random charts.

SMT (Smart Money Technique divergence) compares highs and lows between two instruments that often show related behaviour. Gold traders sometimes compare XAU/USD with DXY or another relevant market, but the relationship is not constant and can change.

Do not treat inverse correlation as a rule

Gold and the US dollar often move in opposite directions, but not always. Choose a comparison that makes sense for the period and use the same timeframe and comparable swing points.

02

Example of possible bearish divergence

Gold makes a higher high, but the related market does not confirm the expected move.

Illustrative values
XAU/USD: previous high2 000
XAU/USD: new high2 015 · HH
DXY: previous low100.0
DXY: new low100.4 · no LL

Gold rises to a new high, but the dollar does not weaken to a new low. This divergence may suggest that gold’s upward move is not confirmed by the other market.

This does not mean gold must fall. It simply tells us to be more cautious and look for additional confirmation before considering a SELL.

03

Example of possible bullish divergence

Gold makes a lower low, but the dollar does not confirm the expected strengthening.

Illustrative values
XAU/USD: previous low1 950
XAU/USD: new low1 940 · LL
DXY: previous high105.0
DXY: new high104.6 · no HH

Gold makes a new low, but the dollar does not reach a new high. This may suggest that selling pressure on gold is weakening.

This is not an automatic BUY. Wait for market structure to improve and for confirmation to appear.

04

How to use SMT?

As an additional clue alongside the main analysis.

  1. Choose two logically related instruments.Do not compare markets that have no clear relationship.
  2. Find comparable swing highs or lows.Use a similar time period and timeframe.
  3. Check whether one market fails to confirm the other.Look for a new HH/LL in one market without confirmation from the other.
  4. Wait for confirmation on the instrument you plan to trade.Use structure, BOS, a retest or another entry model that you have tested in advance.
SMT is confluence, not an entry

Divergence may make an area more interesting, but it does not provide an exact BUY or SELL entry by itself, and it may occur without a subsequent reversal.

Quick check

Gold makes a new high while DXY does not make a new low. Does this guarantee that gold will fall?

Answer: No. It is a possible divergence that requires additional confirmation.
